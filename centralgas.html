Central Gas System Installation



Energy Next excels in central gas system installation, delivering turnkey Natural Gas (NG), Synthetic Natural Gas (SNG), and Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) networks for projects of every scale. From multi-tower residential complexes and five-star hotels to expansive industrial facilities and healthcare campuses, our certified engineers ensure seamless integration of cylinder manifold systems, high-accuracy regulators, and advanced gas leak detection systems.



Harnessing robust project management methodologies and cutting-edge installation practices, we guarantee:
  • Precision pipe routing with CAD-validated layouts
  • Modular skid assembly for off-site pre-fabrication
  • Automated safety controls featuring IoT-enabled sensors
  • Full compliance with UAE fire, environmental, and pressure-vessel codes
Certified to ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, and ISO 45001:2018, our teams adhere to the strictest quality and safety standards, minimizing downtime and delivering projects on-budget and on-schedule.

Commercial Gas Pipeline Installation


As a trusted partner for commercial gas pipeline installation, Energy Next designs and installs centralized LPG and NG mains for retail malls, office towers, and hospitality venues. We optimize pipeline diameters and regulator staging to maintain consistent pressure—even during peak cooking or heating loads—protecting critical equipment and enhancing occupant comfort. Our scope includes:


  • Bulk storage and distribution manifolds
  • High-efficiency vaporizer skids
  • Leak-proof welded and fusion-bonded joints
  • Real-time pressure-monitoring dashboards

By conducting detailed hydraulic modeling and site-specific risk assessments, we mitigate leak and overpressure hazards before they arise—ensuring business continuity and regulatory peace of mind.

Tailored Installations for All Sectors



Residential Projects

From luxury villas to high-rise developments, our residential installations feature discreet “no-dig” trenchless techniques and compact cylinder rack systems. Each home is fitted with individual line regulators and excess-flow valves, providing uninterrupted cooking and heating supply in every unit.

Hospitality & Retail

Our turnkey solution for hotels, restaurants, and shopping centers combines redundant manifold loops with zoned metering. This design allows maintenance work on one section without disrupting overall supply, safeguarding guest comfort and operational revenue.

Industrial Facilities

For factories, laboratories, and processing plants, we deploy large-bore piping and looped distribution networks. Integrated gas-detection sensors feed into programmable logic controllers (PLCs), triggering automated shutdowns and alert notifications at the first sign of anomaly.
