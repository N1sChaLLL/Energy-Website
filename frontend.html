Gas Distribution Services

Commercial Gas Feed

Energy Next’s commercial gas feed expertise ensures reliable, metered LPG, SNG, or NG supply for hotels, hospitals, office towers, and retail complexes. We optimize feed-line sizing, regulator staging, and manifold configurations to maintain steady pressure—even during peak usage. Our detailed load-calculation studies and automated balancing controls reduce pressure drops, protect sensitive appliances, and safeguard tenants.

Industrial Gas Feed

For heavy-duty applications—manufacturing plants, processing facilities, and large-scale warehouses—our industrial gas feed solutions deliver high-volume flow with uncompromising safety. We engineer skid-mounted vaporization units, high-capacity manifolds, and looped piping networks to support 24/7 operations. Integrated leak-detection sensors and remote-monitoring platforms allow real-time diagnostics and rapid fault response.

Residential Gas Feed

Our residential gas feed systems bring safe, low-pressure LPG or natural gas directly to each apartment or villa. Utilizing compact cylinder-rack assemblies and individual line regulators, we create discreet, “no-dig” installations that preserve landscaping integrity. Comprehensive network audits and monthly pressure checks ensure every home enjoys uninterrupted cooking and heating gas supply.

Feed Process Overview


  1. Site & Regulatory Assessment – Gather architectural plans, review zoning and fire codes, and verify utility interfaces.

  2. Flow & Pressure Analysis – Calculate peak and continuous demand, model network hydraulics, and specify regulator sets.

  3. Layout & Material Selection – Design pipe routes in 3D CAD, choose corrosion-resistant materials (stainless steel, PE-RT), and plan manifold skid footprints.

  4. Cost Estimation & Risk Mitigation - Cost Estimation & Risk Mitigation – Provide line-item budgets, identify scope gaps, and outline contingency strategies for schedule or material delays.

  5. Deliverables & Tender Package – Issue comprehensive FEED package—P&IDs, isometrics, equipment data sheets, and bid-ready specification booklets.
