Gas Project Consultation & Management



Energy Next’s gas project management consultancy transforms your conceptual gas infrastructure plans into turnkey, compliant systems—on time and within budget. Leveraging deep expertise in international gas codes and UAE regulations, we deliver data-driven roadmaps that minimize risk, optimize cost, and set clear benchmarks for every project phase.

Sustainable Engineering Solutions

Our sustainable engineering solutions ensure your gas network not only meets current demands but scales for tomorrow’s growth. Through value engineering and life-cycle analysis, we identify material efficiencies, streamline pipe routing, and reduce energy losses—delivering greener operations without compromising performance.

Round-the-clock support is critical when system faults or leak detections occur. Our dedicated hotline connects you instantly to certified engineers who diagnose issues remotely, dispatch field teams, and coordinate corrective actions—ensuring minimal downtime and safeguarding lives and assets.

Cost-Saving Feasibility & Budget Planning
